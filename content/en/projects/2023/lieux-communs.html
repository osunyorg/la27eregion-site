Governing cities and territories in common (2021–2023)

body: >-

Starting point

Here and there, in France and across Europe, there are forms of collective resource management that fall outside the 'entirely public' or 'entirely private' models. These new forms of collective organisation are part of the public-commons partnership movement, which highlights ways in which residents, local stakeholders and institutions cooperate to develop and enhance local resources within a commons-based approach. They are very often drivers of social innovation, generate new ways of shaping the city, rebuild a sense of community where isolation is felt, help to rethink democracy and create new forms of economic activity.


How can we manage a facility, a natural space or a service of public interest whilst involving local stakeholders - residents, voluntary organisations, economic actors and other public institutions, etc. - more actively? How can we allocate roles, share decision-making and responsibility, and devise more collective management approaches? To what extent do such approaches enable us to formulate new responses to the challenges of revitalisation and social, environmental and democratic transition facing our cities? Following on from the 'Enacting the Commons' initiative, 'Lieux Communs' is an experimental programme designed to help local authorities implement or support these new processes of cooperation at a regional level. Programme carried out with financial support from the Grand Plan d'Investissement.

Method
A learning journey at the heart of democratic and cooperative processes
Lieux Communs initially involved raising awareness and training local authorities and urban policy stakeholders on the challenges and new ways of cooperating to manage a shared resource, through a series of collective thematic workshops. These will be followed by a pilot phase enabling local authorities to take ownership of and test new management tools and forms of collective organisation at a local level (mechanisms for involving residents in the management of a resource, a new legal framework for the provision of public land, arrangements for providing material and human support for citizens' initiatives, etc.).

A pilot test in Sevran
Lieux Communs drew in particular on the pilot scheme carried out in Sevran, 'Lieu Commun Masaryk', from March 2021 to June 2023, in partnership with the housing providers Vilogia and Cdc Habitat and the City of Sevran. It focused on jointly developing and testing new forms of collaboration for the joint planning and management of communal residential spaces with local stakeholders (public bodies, housing providers, residents, and local community and business organisations).

Programme phases

(October 2018 – October 2020)
The 'Enacting the Commons' programme: a learning journey to explore public-commons partnerships in Europe.

March 2021 – June 2023
Pilot project in Sevran with "Lieu Commun Masaryk", rethinking the governance of former collective residential premises converted into third places.

May 2022 – June 2023
In partnership with the National Agency for Urban Regeneration (ANRU), a series of awareness-raising workshops with urban regeneration project managers. The aim was to explore what perspectives and inspiration the 'commons' movement could offer to those involved in urban regeneration, and to what extent it could help shape new ways of working to promote progress in terms of well-being and social, urban or democratic quality in these neighbourhoods.

September 2022 – November 2023
Documentation of the "Territoires en commun" initiative on participatory planning, launched by the National Agency for Territorial Cohesion and the Banque des Territoires.
